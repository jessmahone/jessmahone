## 🗂️ Projects

### [Local News · 100 Communities Database](https://github.com/jessicamahone/local-news-100-communities)
`R` `SQLite` `PostgreSQL` `Data Modeling` `Content Analysis`

Cleaned, normalized, and structured 16,000+ local news stories from 100 U.S. communities into a queryable database. Built to support market analysis of local media ecosystem and content and to demonstrate end-to-end data wrangling, schema design, and SQL skills in a real-world context.

---

### Tri-Cities News Analysis *(in progress)*
`Python` `Llama` `R` `Text Analysis`

A deep dive into local news coverage in the Tri-Cities, TN/VA region and a pilot for an AI-assisted content classifier built with Python and Llama 3.2. The goal: build something generalizable, not just one-off.

---

### News Classifier *(upcoming)*
`Python` `Llama` `NLP`

The generalizable version of the Tri-Cities classifier: a locally-run LLM pipeline for categorizing local news content at scale. Designed to be reusable across communities and research contexts.

My work lives at the intersection of journalism, public interest research, and applied data engineering.
